# German 1

In this course you begin to speak, read and write German. You learn
how German sounds and is spelled, how to greet people and introduce
yourself, how to count and tell the time, and how to make sentences
about your day, your family, food and shopping. By the end you reach
level A1 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages
(CEFR): you can understand and use everyday phrases and simple
sentences.

You do not need to know any German before you start. You need to read
English well. The course teaches the grammar words it uses, like noun
and verb, when it needs them.

There are no recordings yet. The course describes each German sound by
comparing it with English sounds. One early lesson shows how to type
the German letters ä, ö, ü and ß on Zeitlos.

The course has three parts. Do them in order: each part uses words
the parts before it teach.
