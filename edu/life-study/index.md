# Learning How to Learn

Some ways of studying work much better than others, and the ones that
feel easy are often not the ones that work. In this short course you
learn what research shows about memory and practice: testing yourself,
spacing your study out, mixing kinds of practice, and sleeping well.
You also see how learn uses these ideas, and you make a study plan of
your own.

Before you start, you should be able to read at about grade 5 and know
how to answer questions in learn (the course *Using learn*). No other
course comes first.
