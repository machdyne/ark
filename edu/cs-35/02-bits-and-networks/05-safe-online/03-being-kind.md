# Being kind online

```lesson
(objective "Treat people online kindly, and say what to do when someone is unkind to you or to others.")
(minutes 15)
```

The people you meet online are real people, so treat them kindly.

Behind every message is a person with feelings. Unkind words hurt
online, just as they do face to face.

In a written message, people cannot see your face or hear your voice. So a joke can
sound mean when you did not mean it. Before you send something, ask:

- Is it kind?
- Would I say it to this person's face?

```sources
(source "StopBullying.gov, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services: Cyberbullying")
```

```check
(choice
  (ask "Why can a joke in a message sound mean, when the same joke face to face sounds fine?")
  (right "The reader cannot see your face or hear your voice")
  (wrong "Messages make every word meaner"
    "The words are the same. What is missing is your smile and your voice, which show you are joking.")
  (wrong "Jokes are not allowed online"
    "Jokes are fine. Just remember the reader cannot see your face, so make sure it cannot hurt.")
  (hint "What do you see and hear when a friend jokes face to face?"))
```

---

## If someone is unkind to you

If someone online is unkind to you, it is not your fault. Here is what
to do:

1. Do not answer back in an unkind way.
2. Keep the messages: do not delete them. They show what happened.
3. Block the person, if the app lets you.
4. Tell a trusted adult.

```check
(choice
  (ask "Someone keeps sending Ava mean messages in a game. What should she do first?")
  (right "Not answer back, keep the messages and tell a trusted adult")
  (wrong "Send mean messages back"
    "Answering back in an unkind way often makes it worse. Keep the messages and tell a trusted adult.")
  (wrong "Delete the messages and say nothing"
    "The messages show what happened, so keep them. And telling a trusted adult means Ava does not have to deal with it alone.")
  (hint "Look at the list: what to do, and who to tell."))
```

---

## If you see it happen to someone else

Sometimes you see someone else being treated unkindly. You can help.
Do not join in or pass the messages on. Be kind to the person it is
happening to. Tell a trusted adult.

```check
(choice
  (ask "In a group chat, some children make fun of a classmate. What helps?")
  (right "Not joining in, being kind to the classmate and telling a trusted adult")
  (wrong "Laughing along, so they do not make fun of you"
    "Laughing along makes it worse for the classmate. You can help by not joining in and by telling a trusted adult.")
  (wrong "Sharing the messages with more friends"
    "Passing the messages on spreads the hurt. Do not share them. Tell a trusted adult instead.")
  (hint "What would you want others to do if it happened to you?"))
```

## Worked example

```example
(example
  (problem "A player Tom does not know writes to him: \"You are so bad. Quit the game.\" What can Tom do?")
  (step "He does not write anything mean back.")
  (step "He keeps the message, so a grown-up can see it.")
  (step "He blocks the player.")
  (answer "And he tells a trusted adult, like his dad."))
```

```example
(example
  (problem "Zoe wants to send her friend: \"Your drawing is so weird, ha ha.\" She means it as a joke. What should she ask herself first?")
  (step "Her friend cannot see her smile.")
  (step (choice (ask "Which question helps Zoe most?")
    (right "Is it kind, and would I say it to her face?")
    (wrong "Will it make the others laugh?" "A laugh for others can still hurt her friend. Ask if it is kind.")))
  (answer "Zoe can say what she likes about the drawing instead, so it cannot be read the wrong way."))
```

## Practice

```check
(choice
  (several)
  (ask "Which of these are kind ways to act online? Choose every one.")
  (right "Asking yourself: would I say this to their face?")
  (right "Saying something nice about a friend's drawing")
  (wrong "Sharing a friend's embarrassing photo without asking"
    "That can hurt and embarrass your friend. Always ask before you share a picture of someone.")
  (hint "Which ones would make the other person feel good, or keep them safe?"))
```

So: be as kind online as face to face, and if anyone is unkind, keep
the messages and tell a trusted adult.
