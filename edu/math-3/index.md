# Grade 3 Math

In this course you learn what multiplying and dividing mean. You learn
all the facts up to 10 x 10. You learn fractions and how to show them
on a number line. You find the area and perimeter of shapes. You tell
time to the minute, measure mass and liquid volume, read graphs and
sort shapes.

Before you start, you should know grade 2 math:

- adding and subtracting within 100, and within 1000 with blocks or drawings
- hundreds, tens and ones up to 1000
- skip counting by 2s, 5s, 10s and 100s
- whole numbers on a number line
- even and odd numbers
- telling time to 5 minutes
- measuring in inches, feet, centimeters and meters
- picture graphs, bar graphs and line plots where each mark counts 1
- halves, thirds and fourths of a shape
- rectangles, squares and triangles

The course has three parts. Do them in order: each part uses words
the parts before it teach.
