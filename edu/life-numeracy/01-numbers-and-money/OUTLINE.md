# Outline: Numbers and money

Part `01-numbers-and-money` of `life-numeracy`, Everyday Maths (level
life, adults, class A): 5 units, 25 lessons. This file has no number,
so learn ignores it. Life courses carry no standard codes.

Every term below is taught in bold, in its lesson's `keywords`, in the
step where it first appears. Part 2 (`02-measures-and-charts`) may use
all of them freely.

## Figures

Own procedures in `lib/nu1.scm`, every name starting with `nu1-`:
`nu1-thermo` (a vertical thermometer scale with a column up to a
value), `nu1-hundred` (a 10 by 10 square with n squares shaded),
`nu1-strip` (a strip of equal parts in two patterns, for ratios).
Built-in procedures used: `number-line`, `nl-mark`, `nl-jump`,
`frac-bar`, `frac-bar-at`, `label`, `caption`.

## Words

- Money in dollars, written `\$` in words; pounds and euros only as
  examples of other currencies. Tax rates are made-up examples and say so.
- No financial advice.
- Not used in part 1, because part 2 teaches them: average, mean,
  median, range (of data), area, chart words (bar chart, line chart,
  pie chart, axis, scale of a chart), the 24-hour clock, converting
  units. Unit names (gram, kilogram, ounce, pound, liter, quart) appear
  only as the words on a price label, never converted; part 2 is free
  to teach them as terms.
- Part 1 avoids every keyword in part 2's OUTLINE (mean, key, unit,
  scale before 04/03, table, data, row, column, convert, pound,
  ounce, gram, liter, cup ...). Unit prices are per item (per can, per
  roll), not per weight. Two shared terms: `05-negative-numbers.md`
  teaches "degree (of temperature)" (only as "the steps on a
  thermometer"; Celsius and Fahrenheit are left to part 2), and
  `04/03-recipes.md` teaches "scale (a recipe)" -- both are "scale" to
  terms.py, so part 2's "scale (of a measuring tool)" comes after it.
- `try` blocks open `calc` (a four-function calculator in the dock's
  Desk group: no percent key; Enter is equals).

## 01-big-numbers/ -- Big numbers and negative numbers

Read, round and estimate with whole numbers, and use negative numbers.

1. `01-place-value.md` -- Read and write whole numbers using the value of each digit's place.
   Keywords: "digit", "place value".
2. `02-millions.md` -- Read and write numbers in the thousands, millions and billions, in figures and in words.
   Keywords: "million", "billion".
3. `03-rounding.md` -- Round a whole number to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000.
   Keywords: "round (a number)".
4. `04-estimating.md` -- Estimate a sum or a product by rounding first, and use it to check a calculator answer.
   Keywords: "estimate".
5. `05-negative-numbers.md` -- Read, compare and change negative numbers on a thermometer and a bank balance.
   Keywords: "negative number", "balance (of an account)", "overdrawn".

## 02-fractions-decimals/ -- Fractions and decimals

Use fractions and decimals for parts of a whole and parts of an amount.

1. `01-fractions.md` -- Write a fraction for part of a whole and say what its two numbers mean.
   Keywords: "fraction", "numerator", "denominator".
2. `02-fraction-of-an-amount.md` -- Find a fraction of an amount: divide by the bottom, multiply by the top.
   Keywords: (none new).
3. `03-equivalent-fractions.md` -- Simplify a fraction and tell when two fractions are equal.
   Keywords: "equivalent fractions", "simplest form".
4. `04-decimals.md` -- Read decimals as tenths and hundredths, as in prices.
   Keywords: "decimal", "decimal point", "tenth", "hundredth".
5. `05-fractions-to-decimals.md` -- Change a fraction to a decimal by dividing, and compare decimals.
   Keywords: (none new).

## 03-percentages/ -- Percentages

Use percentages, move between fractions, decimals and percentages, and

1. `01-percent.md` -- Read a percentage as a number out of 100.
   Keywords: "percent", "percentage".
2. `02-fractions-decimals-percents.md` -- Change between fractions, decimals and percentages.
   Keywords: (none new).
3. `03-percent-of-an-amount.md` -- Find a percentage of an amount, in your head (10%, 5%, 50%, 25%) and with a calculator.

   Keywords: "discount".
5. `05-sales-tax.md` -- Add sales tax or VAT given as a percentage.
