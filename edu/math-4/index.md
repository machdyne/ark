# Grade 4 Math

In this course you work with numbers up to a million. You add and
subtract big numbers, multiply numbers with more digits, and divide
with remainders. You find factors, multiples and prime numbers, and
follow patterns. You learn which fractions are equal, add and subtract
fractions, and write tenths and hundredths as decimals. You convert
units, find area and perimeter, and measure and draw angles.

Before you start, you should know grade 3 math (the course Grade 3
Math):

- multiplication and division facts up to 10 x 10
- adding and subtracting within 1000
- rounding to the nearest 10 or 100
- unit fractions and fractions on a number line
- area and perimeter of rectangles
- telling time to the minute

The course has four parts. Do them in order: each part uses words the
parts before it teach.
