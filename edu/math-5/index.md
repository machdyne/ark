# Grade 5 Math

In this course you see how each place is ten times the one to its
right, write powers of ten, and work with decimals to thousandths. You
add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals, multiply and divide
bigger whole numbers, and write and read expressions. You add and
subtract fractions with different denominators, and multiply and
divide with fractions. You find the volume of boxes, plot points on a
coordinate grid, and sort shapes by their properties.

Before you start, you should know grade 4 math (the course Grade 4
Math):

- place value to a million, and multiplying and dividing whole numbers
- factors, multiples and equivalent fractions
- adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator
- tenths and hundredths as decimals
- area, perimeter, angles and lines

The course has four parts. Do them in order: each part uses words the
parts before it teach.
