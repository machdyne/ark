# Knots

A good knot holds when you need it to and comes undone when you want it
to. In this course you learn the names for the parts of a rope, the
four kinds of knot, and a dozen useful knots, each drawn step by step.
Have a piece of rope or string beside you and tie each knot as you go.

Knots for climbing, or for lifting or holding a person, are not in
this course: learn those from a qualified instructor.
