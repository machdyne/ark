# High School Physics

In this course you learn how things move and why: motion, forces,
gravity and orbits. You learn how energy and momentum are shared and
kept, how things oscillate, and how waves carry sound and light. Then
you learn about electric circuits, magnetism, photons and the nucleus.
Every experiment is a simulation you can change and watch.

Before you start, you should know:

- middle school physical science: matter is made of atoms; pushes and pulls change motion; energy, waves and magnets at a first look
- arithmetic with decimals, fractions, percents and negative numbers
- algebra 1 helps a great deal: solving and rearranging equations, graphs of lines and their slope, squares and square roots, and powers of 10

The course teaches the little trigonometry it uses (sine and cosine)
when it needs it. Units are SI (meters, kilograms, seconds), with US
customary units alongside where they are natural.

The course has three parts. Do them in order: each part uses words
the parts before it teach.
