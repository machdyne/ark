# Outline: Thinking

Part `01-thinking` of `life-think`, Thinking Clearly: 6 units, 28 lessons. This file has no number, so learn ignores it. Level `life`: no standard codes. Each lesson has exactly the file name and objective below; the keywords are the terms it teaches, each defined in bold in the step where it first appears.

## Figures

Own procedures in `lib/th.scm` only, every name starting with `th-`: `th-people` (100 people as a 10 x 10 grid, some shaded), `th-node` (a box of words for a tree), `th-bars` (a bar chart whose axis can start above 0, for misleading charts). Built-in procedures that suit: `bar-chart`, `graph`, `graph-axes`, `plot-point`, `frac-bar`, `arrow`, `label`, `caption`.

## Words and care

- Plain adult English, ages 12+: sentences of about 20 words, "you", present tense, numbers as digits.
- Learners should know fractions, decimals and percentages (about grade 6).
- Class D care: every example is neutral and everyday (sports, shopping, food, school, made-up towns). No side is taken on any live political, religious or moral question.
- Every number in an example is made up and says so ("Suppose ..."). No invented studies, statistics or quotations.
- Do not use "fallacy" before unit 2, "probability" before unit 3, "correlation" before unit 4, "cognitive bias" before unit 6.

## 01-arguments/ -- Arguments

Take an argument apart into its claims, reasons, premises and conclusion, and tell valid from sound.

1. `01-claims-and-reasons.md` -- Tell a claim from a reason and an argument from a mere statement. Keywords: claim, reason, argument.
2. `02-premises-and-conclusions.md` -- Find the premises and the conclusion of a short argument. Keywords: premise, conclusion, indicator word.
3. `03-hidden-premises.md` -- Find the unstated premise an argument needs. Keywords: hidden premise.
4. `04-valid-and-sound.md` -- Tell whether an argument is valid, and whether it is also sound. Keywords: valid, sound.

## 02-fallacies/ -- Fallacies

Name a dozen common fallacies in short everyday exchanges, and say what is wrong with each.

1. `01-straw-man-ad-hominem.md` -- Spot a straw man and an ad hominem. Keywords: fallacy, straw man, ad hominem.
2. `02-false-dilemma-slippery-slope.md` -- Spot a false dilemma and a slippery slope. Keywords: false dilemma, slippery slope.
3. `03-popularity-and-authority.md` -- Spot an appeal to popularity, and tell a fair appeal to authority from a fallacious one. Keywords: appeal to popularity, appeal to authority.
4. `04-post-hoc-hasty.md` -- Spot post hoc reasoning and a hasty generalization (US spelling). Keywords: post hoc, hasty generalization.
5. `05-circular-red-herring.md` -- Spot circular reasoning and a red herring. Keywords: circular reasoning, red herring.
6. `06-moving-the-goalposts.md` -- Spot moving the goalposts, and name any of the 12 fallacies in a mixed set. Keywords: moving the goalposts, fallacy fallacy.

## 03-chance-and-risk/ -- Chance and risk

Think about chance as a fraction, and judge a risk by its base rate and its absolute size.

1. `01-probability.md` -- Give the probability of an event as a fraction, decimal and percent. Keywords: probability, outcome, event.
2. `02-gamblers-fallacy.md` -- Explain why past results of independent events do not change the next one. Keywords: independent (events), gambler's fallacy.
3. `03-relative-and-absolute-risk.md` -- Turn a relative risk into an absolute one, and see which a headline uses. Keywords: risk, absolute risk, relative risk.
4. `04-base-rates.md` -- Use a base rate and a frequency tree to judge what a positive test means. Keywords: base rate, false positive, natural frequency.

## 04-numbers-in-the-news/ -- Numbers in the news

Read percentages, averages, samples, links between things, and charts in the news without being misled.

1. `01-percent-and-points.md` -- Tell a change in percent from a change in percentage points. Keywords: percentage point.
2. `02-averages.md` -- Choose between the mean and the median, and see how an outlier moves the mean. Keywords: the mean (so the verb "mean" stays free), median, outlier.
3. `03-samples-and-margins.md` -- Judge a survey by its sample and read its margin of error. Keywords: sample, population (of a survey), random sample, biased sample, margin of error.
4. `04-correlation-and-causation.md` -- Tell correlation from causation and find a possible third cause. Keywords: correlation, causation, confounding factor.
5. `05-misleading-charts.md` -- Spot a cut axis and other tricks in a chart. Keywords: axis (of a chart), truncated axis.

## 05-sources-and-science/ -- Sources and science

Judge a source by who made it and what evidence it gives, and explain how science tests ideas.

1. `01-who-and-evidence.md` -- Ask who made a source, why, and what evidence it gives. Keywords: source (of information). "Evidence" is defined in bold here but is an everyday word, used from unit 2.
2. `02-lateral-reading.md` -- Check a website by reading what others say about it. Keywords: lateral reading.
3. `03-primary-and-secondary.md` -- Tell a primary from a secondary source and say what each is good for. Keywords: primary source, secondary source.
4. `04-how-science-tests.md` -- Describe how science moves from observation to hypothesis to a fair test. Keywords: observation, hypothesis, fair test, control group.
5. `05-peer-review.md` -- Explain peer review and why one study is not the last word. Keywords: peer review, replication.

## 06-biases/ -- Biases

Notice four biases everyone has, and use a habit against each.

1. `01-confirmation-bias.md` -- Notice confirmation bias and test an idea by looking for what would show it wrong. Keywords: cognitive bias, confirmation bias.
2. `02-anchoring.md` -- Notice how a first number pulls later guesses. Keywords: anchoring.
3. `03-availability.md` -- Notice how easy-to-remember events seem more common than they are. Keywords: availability heuristic.
4. `04-survivorship.md` -- Notice when only the survivors are counted, and close the course. Keywords: survivorship bias.
