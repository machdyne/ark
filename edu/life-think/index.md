# Thinking Clearly

Everyone meets arguments, numbers and claims every day, and everyone
can be fooled -- by others and by their own mind. In this course you
learn to take an argument apart, to name the common tricks and slips,
to think about chance and risk, to read the numbers in the news, to
check where a claim comes from, and to notice the biases all of us
share. The course does not tell you what to believe; it helps you
decide for yourself.

Before you start, you should be able to work with fractions, decimals
and percentages (about grade 6).
